While you wait

Water going everywhere? Do this first.

  1. Shut the water off. Main valve is usually where the line enters the house, or at the meter by the street.
  2. Kill power to the area if water is anywhere near outlets, the panel, or a water heater.
  3. Open a low faucet to drain the pressure out of the pipes.
  4. Then call us. Tell us what you see — we'll tell you what it'll take.

Before you call

Straight answers

Are you really open at 2 in the morning?

Yes. Plumbing does not keep business hours and neither do we. Call the same number any hour, any day — including weekends and holidays.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Estimates are free. Tell us what is happening and we will tell you what it is likely to take before any work starts.

Are you licensed and insured?

Licensed, bonded and insured, and a registered Virginia LLC. Ask to see any of it and we will show you.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes — shops, offices, rentals and restaurants as well as houses. Same number.

Will you come into North Carolina?

We do, regularly. Caswell and Rockingham counties are a short run from Danville. Call and ask about your address.

What should I do before you get here?

Shut the water off at the main valve, kill the power if water is near anything electrical, and open a low faucet to drain the pressure. Then call.

Got a leak, a clog, or no hot water?

One call. A real plumber. Usually the same day.
