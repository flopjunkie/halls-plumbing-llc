Well service

Well pumps and pressure tanks

Many homes in this area get their water from a well. The pump, pressure tank, switch, wiring and the line to the house all have to work together, and when one part fails, the whole house can lose water.

We service all of it, including submersible pumps — and we pull and replace pumps ourselves. You won't be stuck waiting on a second company before your water comes back on.

If you have no water, the pump won't shut off, or your pressure has dropped, give us a call and describe what's happening. We can often narrow down the problem over the phone.

Call about your well
  • Submersible pump replacement The well job we're called for most. We pull the old pump and install the new one ourselves.
  • Pressure tanks & switches A waterlogged tank or a worn switch can cause the pump to cut on and off repeatedly.
  • No water at all The pump won't come on, the breaker keeps tripping, or nothing comes out of the tap. We find the cause and fix it.
  • Low water pressure Usually caused by the pressure tank, the switch or the pump.
  • The line from the well to the house Leaks and breaks in the buried line between the wellhead and the building.
  • New hookups Well system hookups for new construction, additions and replacement buildings.

Before we arrive

Have a leak? Do this first.

  1. Shut the water off. The main shut‑off is usually where the water line enters the house, or at the meter near the street.
  2. Turn off the power to the area if water is near outlets, the electrical panel or a water heater.
  3. Open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pressure from the pipes.
  4. Then call us. Describe what you see and we'll tell you what to expect.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge for an estimate?

No, estimates are free. We'll tell you what the job will take before any work starts.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed, bonded and insured, and a registered Virginia LLC. We're happy to show proof on request.

Do you work on commercial buildings?

Yes. We work on shops, offices, rental properties and restaurants as well as homes.

My house is on a well — can you work on it?

Yes. We work on submersible pumps, pressure tanks, pressure switches and the line from the well to the house, and we pull pumps ourselves. If you've lost water or pressure, call and describe the problem. We can often narrow it down over the phone.

Will you come into North Carolina?

Yes. We regularly work in Caswell and Rockingham counties. Call to check whether we cover your address.

What should I do before you get here?

Shut off the water at the main valve, turn off the power if water is near anything electrical, and open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pressure. Then give us a call.
