What we do

Residential & commercial plumbing

If it carries water, we work on it. Straight pricing, clean work, no upsell.

Emergency repairs

Burst and frozen pipes, major leaks, no water, backed-up lines. Day, night, weekend or holiday.

Drain cleaning

Slow sinks, clogged toilets, tubs that won't drain, kitchen lines and main sewer stoppages cleared.

Water heaters

Repair or replacement, tank and tankless. No hot water, running out fast, leaking at the base.

New installation

New construction, additions, remodels and re-pipes. Fixtures, supply lines, drains and vents.

Pipe & sewer lines

Leak location, section replacement, and repairs on supply and waste lines under the house or in the yard.

Fixtures & remodels

Faucets, toilets, sinks, tubs, showers, garbage disposals and outside spigots — swapped or rebuilt.

While you wait

Water going everywhere? Do this first.

  1. Shut the water off. Main valve is usually where the line enters the house, or at the meter by the street.
  2. Kill power to the area if water is anywhere near outlets, the panel, or a water heater.
  3. Open a low faucet to drain the pressure out of the pipes.
  4. Then call us. Tell us what you see — we'll tell you what it'll take.

About Hall's

Jerry Hall has been under these houses for thirty years.

Hall's Plumbing has been a licensed Virginia LLC since August 2013, but the trade in this family goes back a lot further than the paperwork does. It is a small, locally owned shop in Danville — which means the man who answers the phone is the man who shows up, and he has to see you at the grocery store afterward.

That is the whole business model: honest pricing, tidy work, and picking up when it's late and something has gone badly wrong.
